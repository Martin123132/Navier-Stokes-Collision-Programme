# Annular two-shear square gate

## 1. Result

The original one-shear annular witness reduced its leading four-high
coefficient to a difference of two Euler component curvatures. Its
numerical value is stably negative, but its sign still requires an interval
certificate.

There is an additive replacement branch that removes that sign problem.
Change both the high polarization and the low field:

```text
b(x,y,z)
 =S(x,y,z)*(x^2+y^2)/(x*r^3)*(-z,0,x),

S=sin(pi(x-2))*cos(pi*y)*cos(pi*z),
r^2=x^2+y^2+z^2,

(x,y,z) in D=[2,3]x[-1/2,1/2]^2.
```

Extend `b` evenly to `-D` and by zero elsewhere. Use the sum of the two low
shears

```text
ell_yz=(0,1,-1),   d_yz=(0,1,1)/sqrt(2),
ell_xy=(1,-1,0),   d_xy=(-1,-1,0)/sqrt(2).
```

For this modified witness, the static HHL continuum load and the leading
four-high continuum coefficient are both strictly negative by exact
identities:

```text
b_0,*=-(sqrt(2)/20)*||b||_L2(D)^2<0,             (1.1)

L_*=-(3*sqrt(2)/20)*||v_y||_2^2<0.              (1.2)
```

No numerical sign estimate or interval quadrature is used in (1.1)-(1.2).
The exact odd finite packet is defined and replayed below. The follow-up
`annular_two_shear_full_c1_port.md` closes its profile convergence and
fourteen-profile amplitude-one tail. The optimizer and time-window estimates
remain open.

## 2. Admissible high profile

The profile has components

```text
b_x=-S*z*(x^2+y^2)/(x*r^3),
b_y=0,
b_z= S*(x^2+y^2)/r^3.
```

It is pointwise divergence free:

```text
(x,y,z) dot b
 =S*(x^2+y^2)/(x*r^3)*(-xz+xz)
 =0.                                             (2.1)
```

The sine and cosine factors vanish on all six faces of `D`. Since `x>=2`,
all rational multipliers and their first derivatives are bounded. The zero
extension is therefore compactly supported and belongs to `H^1`. The even
extension preserves (2.1) on `-D`.

This is a legal three-dimensional packet profile, not a Cartesian
component split of the old profile. In particular, incompressibility holds
for the modified profile by itself.

## 3. Exact two-shear matrix

Keep the existing vertex weight

```text
w_s=product_j (1/2 if s_j=0, 1/4 otherwise),
s in {-1,0,1}^3,
```

and the parity phase

```text
sigma_q=(-1)^(q_x+q_y+q_z).
```

For a low pair `(ell,d=s/sqrt(2))`, the coefficient of
`U dot grad Phi` is

```text
A_q=(s dot q)[w_(q-ell)-w_(q+ell)]/sqrt(2).
```

Exact rational enumeration gives

```text
Q_yz=(sqrt(2)/40) diag(0,-1,1),
Q_xy=(sqrt(2)/40) diag(1,-1,0).
```

There are 36 active outputs for each shear and 58 after combining overlaps.
Every active output has `|q|^2<=6`. Their sum is

```text
Q_*=(sqrt(2)/40) diag(1,-2,1).                  (3.1)
```

The low strain matrices, after removing the common `sqrt(2)` normalization,
are

```text
S_yz=diag(0,1,-1),
S_xy=diag(-1,1,0),
S_*=diag(-1,2,-1).                               (3.2)
```

The fixed-output and static matrices satisfy the exact relation

```text
sqrt(2)*Q_*=-S_*/20.                             (3.3)
```

Both low waves have even coordinate sum, so the parity and finite-difference
mechanisms used by the existing tail ledger remain available. The combined
stencil has exact pre-normalization `l1` mass `3`, twice the old value.

## 4. Static sign

The existing pressure-only HHL calculation is linear in the low field.
Coordinate permutation and the sign of the low polarization give

```text
b_0,yz=(sqrt(2)/20) integral_D (b_y^2-b_z^2),
b_0,xy=(sqrt(2)/20) integral_D (b_y^2-b_x^2).
```

Since `b_y=0`,

```text
b_0,*=b_0,yz+b_0,xy
     =-(sqrt(2)/20) integral_D (b_x^2+b_z^2)
     =-(sqrt(2)/20)||b||_L2(D)^2<0.
```

The strict inequality follows because `b_z` is nonzero in the interior of
`D`.

## 5. Four-high square identity

Let `v` be the first Euler coefficient generated by `b`, and let `gamma` be
the real even second Taylor coefficient in the sign convention of the
dealiased audit. Define

```text
C_j=integral |v_j|^2+2 integral gamma_j*b_j.
```

Euler energy conservation gives

```text
C_x+C_y+C_z=0.                                   (5.1)
```

Because `b_y=0`,

```text
C_y=integral |v_y|^2.                            (5.2)
```

The `yz` fixed-output matrix contributes

```text
L_yz=(sqrt(2)/20)(C_z-C_y),
```

and the sign-flipped `xy` matrix contributes

```text
L_xy=(sqrt(2)/20)(C_x-C_y).
```

Adding and using (5.1)-(5.2),

```text
L_*
 =(sqrt(2)/20)(C_x+C_z-2C_y)
 =-(3*sqrt(2)/20) C_y
 =-(3*sqrt(2)/20)||v_y||_2^2.                   (5.3)
```

Thus only strict nonvanishing of `v_y` remains.

## 6. Strict nonvanishing

Define the convolution matrix

```text
A(rho)=integral b(rho-xi) tensor b(xi) dxi,
M=A(0)=integral b tensor b.
```

The same `H^1` convolution argument used in the Leray-cusp audit gives

```text
A(rho)=M+O(|rho|^2).                              (6.1)
```

Parity in `z`, together with `b_y=0`, gives

```text
M=diag(m_x,0,m_z),   m_z=integral b_z^2>0.
```

For `rho=t(0,1,1)`,

```text
(P_rho M rho)_y=-m_z*t/2.
```

Since `v(rho)=P_rho A(rho)rho`, equation (6.1) yields

```text
v_y(t(0,1,1))=-m_z*t/2+O(t^3).                   (6.2)
```

It is nonzero for all sufficiently small nonzero `t`. Continuity then
implies `||v_y||_2^2>0`, completing the analytic sign proof in (5.3).

At every size:

For odd `N`, define the positive packet on


```text

 =parity(k)*sine_N(k)
```
