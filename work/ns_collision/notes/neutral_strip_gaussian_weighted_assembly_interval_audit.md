# Gaussian-weighted assembly interval audit

## Scope

This stage encloses the exact weighted P1 finite-element forms on the stored
binary polygon around the q12 matrices used by the `h=0.06`, `K=240` frozen
calculation. It treats every stored binary64 vertex as an exact real input.
It does not replace the polygon by the true circular domain.

For each stored triangle `T`, the enclosed quantities are

```text
M_T,ij = integral_T exp(-x^2/2) lambda_i lambda_j,
A_T,ij = integral_T exp(-x^2/2) grad(lambda_i).grad(lambda_j),
```

together with the state-to-inner-boundary stiffness and mass couplings.

## Directed geometry

The signed triangle determinant, inverse edge map, and gradient Gram matrix
are evaluated with outward IEEE-754 intervals. Every one of the 30,954
determinant intervals stays strictly away from zero:

```text
minimum determinant lower bound       0.0011222532900354307
maximum determinant interval width    8.673617379884035e-18.
```

Thus all inverse maps and gradient intervals used below are well-defined for
the exact stored vertices.

## Gaussian moments

On one triangle choose a binary64 center `c` and write

```text
x = c + z,       z = sum_i z_i lambda_i.
```

The Gaussian Taylor coefficients are generated by

```text
a_0 = exp(-c^2/2),
a_1 = -c a_0,
(n+1) a_(n+1) = -c a_n - a_(n-1).
```

The initial exponential is evaluated by `mpmath.iv`; all recurrence and
moment arithmetic is carried by outward balls.

For barycentric exponents `p=(p_0,p_1,p_2)`, set
`alpha_i=p_i+1` and `alpha=sum_i alpha_i`. If `S_n` is the coefficient of
`t^n` in

```text
product_i (1-z_i t)^(-alpha_i),
```

then the exact polynomial moment is

```text
integral_T lambda^p z^n
 = |det(T)| product_i(p_i!)/(sum_i p_i+2)!
   * n!/(alpha)_n * S_n.                                (1)
```

Formula (1) is evaluated with directed convolution. Degree 22 is used for
the weighted area in the stiffness form. Degree 20 is used for mass because
the two barycentric factors consume two degrees of the q12 Duffy rule.

The Taylor remainder uses

```text
|d^n exp(-x^2/2)/dx^n|
 <= n! sum_(m=0..floor(n/2))
        X^(n-2m)/(2^m m! (n-2m)!),
```

where `X` is the maximum `|x|` on the triangle. This derivative bound is
multiplied by the exact unweighted barycentric moment and the appropriate
power of the maximum `|z|`.

## Global error bounds

After local error accumulation and duplicate-summation roundoff, the largest
entrywise errors are

```text
mass                  1.2142378297362264e-16
stiffness             3.7787817589959540e-13
boundary stiffness    1.1646544513831305e-13
boundary mass         1.6523696698987665e-17.
```

Let `E_M` be the nonnegative symmetric entrywise error majorant for mass,
`D` the diagonal of stored mass row sums, and

```text
M_q12 >= alpha D,       alpha = 0.14999999999999333.
```

The elementary inequality

```text
2 |x_i x_j| <= x_i^2 + x_j^2
```

gives

```text
|x^T(M_exact-M_q12)x|
 <= x^T E_M x
 <= max_i row_sum(E_M)_i/D_i * x^T D x
 <= epsilon_M x^T M_q12 x.                              (2)
```

The same reduction applies to stiffness with an additive coefficient in
stored-mass units. Directed production values are

```text
epsilon_M                                      5.492564204190142e-13
epsilon_A in stored-mass form units            5.431182629562088e-9
M_exact lower relative to M_q12                0.9999999999994507.
```

Therefore the reference finite-element mass, stiffness, boundary stiffness
coupling, and boundary mass coupling on the stored polygon are interval
enclosed. The assembly error is far below the current spectral and endpoint
budgets.

## What remains open

This result does not certify the broad frozen coefficient block. In
particular, it does not yet enclose:

1. the composed boundary Riesz, push, and cross solves as operators;
2. the effect of assembly and eigensystem residuals on endpoint outputs;
3. off-block leakage, continuum Ritz transfer, or polygon-to-circle domain
   perturbation.

The indexed count and its min-max transfer to the exact forms on this stored
polygon are now closed by
`neutral_strip_sparse_inertia_indexed_spectrum.md`. Eigenvector/projector
transfer remains open.

The boundary coupling entry bounds above must be propagated through the
correct trace norms before they can be charged against the final response.
